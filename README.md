## About Me
I've spent the last 11 years in healthcare technology. I started on the customer-facing side in CX & relationship management. Then I moved into QA, where I also served as Scrum Master. That gave me a real grounding in how software actually gets used and tested before I ever wrote a requirement myself.

From there I moved into Product. At AdvancedMD, I was Product Manager over the patient engagement suite including Telehealth, Patient Portal, electronic payments.

The last two years, I've been at CRIO, on their EDC team. I owned their Reviewer platform and led a roughly year-long integration project transmitting eSource data into EDC systems, including Veeva's Vault CDMS. I stood up fully remote contracted teams based in India with SierraDev. I helped the product team augment product tasks and workflows with AI, utilizing Claude (Cowork, Skills, Code), and integrating with Product tools like Jira, Confluence, Looker, Figma, etc.
